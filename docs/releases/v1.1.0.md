A big update to **MapMaking**: more layouts and palettes, journeys, print PDFs, framed mockups, a cleaner panel and much shorter share links.

**Live app:** https://guilhermelimait.github.io/MapMaking/

### Posters
- New **Heart** layout, alongside Fade, Block, Border and Circle
- **Print PDF** sized exactly in mm for print shops
- **My designs** saves favourite designs in your browser to reopen later

### Map styling
- 27 palettes: 8 light, 7 dark and 12 inspired by games
- **Surprise me** now mixes palette, layout, typeface and sometimes a tilted or rotated view

### Icons and journeys
- Each icon has its own icon, text and outline colours
- Connect icons with a solid, dashed or dotted journey line, straight or curved

### Frames and mockups
- The wall preview shows a black frame by default
- **Buy frame** (next to Export) searches Amazon for a frame in your exact poster size, in your country's store. It is an affiliate link

### Sharing
- Links only store the settings you changed, so they are far shorter
- **Copy link** (or **Link** on iPhone) makes a short is.gd link; if is.gd can't be reached, the full link is copied instead
- Links shared before this release still open

### Panel
- Settings ordered by use, sections start collapsed, icons on section titles
- A reset button with Undo in every section, plus **Reset all settings**
- One consistent button style, a right-side action dock, and a fixed footer with GitHub, Ko-fi and the OpenStreetMap credit
- The panel no longer shifts sideways when its scrollbar appears

### Download
`index.html` is the whole app in one file: open it in Chrome, Edge or Safari on a computer, or host it anywhere.
