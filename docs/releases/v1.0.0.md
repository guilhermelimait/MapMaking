First public release of **MapMaking**, a browser app for designing minimalist city map posters and wallpapers from real OpenStreetMap data.

**Live app:** https://guilhermelimait.github.io/MapMaking/

### Posters
- Four layouts: Fade, Block, Border and Circle
- Title, subtitle, coordinates (DMS, decimal or latitude/longitude) and an optional tagline
- Six typefaces: Montserrat, Jost, Space Mono, Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, Barlow Condensed
- Print sizes A4–A2, 30×40 to 50×70 cm and 8×10 to 24×36 in, portrait or landscape, at 150 or 300 dpi

### Map styling
- 15 palettes (7 light print styles, 8 dark wallpaper styles), every color editable
- Per-road-type visibility and line weight, water, parks, buildings, glow, edge fade
- Rotation, tilt and three street-detail levels

### Icons, mockups and wallpapers
- Icons (pin, heart, star, home, flag, plane, target, dot) with their own color and label
- Framed wall mockups (black, oak, walnut or white frame, six wall colors) exported as PNG
- Wallpaper mode for phone, tablet, handheld and desktop up to 5K
- "Disguise this place" turns a real city into a fictional one
- Every setting is saved in the shareable link

### iPhone
- Full-screen preview with a bottom tab bar, pinch to zoom, Save to Photos
- Add to Home Screen support

### Download
`index.html` is the whole app in one file: open it in Chrome, Edge or Safari on a computer, or host it anywhere.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Tiles by OpenFreeMap. Rendering by MapLibre GL JS (BSD-3-Clause).

Source code, build script and screenshots are in the repository.
