**MapMaking 1.2.0** makes it easier to share designs and get them on your wall.

**Live app:** https://guilhermelimait.github.io/MapMaking/

### Order print
- New **Order print** button next to Buy frame: it opens the MYPOSTER poster page in your country (Italy, Germany, Austria, Switzerland, France, Spain, the Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg or the US). Export your poster, then upload it there.
- After an export, the save window also offers "Order this as a print".

### Shorter share links
- Links only store the settings you changed, so they are far shorter.
- **Copy link** (or **Link** on iPhone) makes a short is.gd link. If is.gd can't be reached, the full link is copied instead.
- Links shared before this release still open.

### README
- New poster gallery of 12 cities and a "Different views" section with tilted perspectives.

### Download
`index.html` is the whole app in one file: open it in Chrome, Edge or Safari on a computer, or host it anywhere.
