A small cleanup release for **MapMaking**.

**Live app:** https://guilhermelimait.github.io/MapMaking/

### Changes
- Exported posters, wallpapers and mockups no longer print a map-data credit in the corner, and the "credit on export" switch is gone from the Text section.
- The OpenStreetMap credit stays in the app footer and in the README. If you share or sell posters, credit "© OpenStreetMap contributors" nearby, for example in the post or listing description.

### Download
`index.html` is the whole app in one file: open it in Chrome, Edge or Safari on a computer, or host it anywhere.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Tiles by OpenFreeMap. Rendering by MapLibre GL JS (BSD-3-Clause).
